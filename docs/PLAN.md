# Tyrelink A-Z build and growth plan

## Product strategy
Position Tyrelink as Sri Lanka's trusted omnichannel tyre and garage platform. The physical operation is the proof: premises, staff, stock handling, invoices, warranty and after-sales ownership should be visible online.

## MVP scope
- Find tyres by vehicle, sidewall size and category.
- Compare three suitable options with plain-language pros/cons.
- Show tyre-only, delivery, fitting, balancing, valve and installed totals separately.
- Show stock timestamp, manufacture period, origin and warranty issuer.
- Reserve online, request confirmation or book fitting.
- Mobile-first WhatsApp and phone support.
- Public launch pricing with WhatsApp quote/order confirmation; no cart or POS dependency.
- Islandwide delivery messaging and 24-hour WhatsApp support.
- Sinhala-first customer education alongside English product data.
- Separate consumer tyre journey from garage-equipment quotation journey.

## UX/UI direction
- Brand: confident black, signal red and warm off-white; technical but human.
- Homepage CTA: “Find my tyre”, supported by “Shop tyres” and “Talk to an expert”.
- Navigation: Shop tyres, Services, For garages, Learn.
- Use good/better/best merchandising; avoid technical codes as the only explanation.
- Every product card answers: will it fit, is it available, how old is it, what is the total price, where can it be fitted?
- Sinhala/English learning content at launch; Tamil can follow after the first content set.

## Architecture decision
Astro is the right starting point for the marketing, catalogue and SEO-heavy surface: excellent static performance, content collections, and small interactive islands. Use React/Svelte islands only for the finder, filters and checkout-like flows. Add a typed API/backend when stock, payments, partner appointments and warranties go live. Next.js is preferable only if the first release is primarily a logged-in transactional application with heavy server-side state.

## SEO moat
- Useful landing pages for vehicle models, tyre sizes, categories and verified fitting towns.
- Internal linking: vehicle → size → product → fitting location → guide.
- Original fitment and tyre-age content, not thin autogenerated pages.
- Local SEO for Tyrelink and verified partners with consistent name, address and phone data.
- Product, LocalBusiness, FAQ, Article and Breadcrumb structured data where truthful.
- Core Web Vitals, image optimization, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots.txt, Search Console and review governance.
- Earn links through safety guides, garage resources, supplier partnerships and local motoring communities.

## Delivery phases
1. Foundation: brand system, catalogue schema, analytics events, content model and technical SEO.
2. MVP: finder, catalogue, quote/reserve flow, WhatsApp, locations and trust pages.
3. Commerce: payments, stock/inventory integration, delivery rules, fitting appointments and warranties.
4. B2B: garage packages, site survey, quotation, installation, training, spare parts and maintenance.
5. Growth: Sinhala/Tamil content, vehicle/size SEO clusters, reminders, saved vehicles and repeat orders.

## Decisions needed from Tyrelink
- Launch geography and first fitting locations.
- First 50–100 SKUs and whether prices are public.
- Payment, delivery, reserve and fitting policy.
- Existing inventory/POS/CRM systems and data access.
- Warranty and manufacture-date rules.
- Official phone, WhatsApp, email, address and business hours.
- Sinhala/Tamil content ownership and brand photography.
