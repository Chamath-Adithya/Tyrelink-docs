# Product and content truth rules

Every public product must have a stable SKU, category, size/specification,
source, reviewer and review date. Keep tyre facts structured: size, tube type,
load index, speed symbol, position, manufacture-date policy and warranty are
separate fields. Garage equipment needs capacity, dimensions, power,
installation, training and support fields.

Until supplier and operations data are connected, all launch prices are
`indicative sample prices`, availability is “confirm on WhatsApp”, and fitment
is never guaranteed. “Islandwide”, “24 hours”, verified stock, fresh DOT date,
fitted total, warranty issuer and customer reviews require evidence and an
owner before publication.

Consumer tyres and garage equipment have separate journeys and CTAs. Sinhala
copy is reviewed by a fluent human; model codes, sizes and standards remain
unchanged. No thin district pages, fake reviews, keyword stuffing or invented
compatibility claims.
